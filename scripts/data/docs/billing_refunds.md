# Billing, Repayments, and Refunds Policy — FlexiPay

**Document version:** 1.0
**Last updated:** 2026-08-01
**Source of truth:** Billing & Customer Operations Policy (internal reference BC-2026-009)

## 1. Billing Cycle and Auto-Debits

FlexiPay plans consist of 3 equal monthly installments. 
*   **First Installment:** Paid immediately at the time of purchase or onboarding checkout.
*   **Second and Third Installments:** Auto-debited on the same calendar date of the subsequent two months (e.g., if checkout is on August 15th, auto-debits occur on September 15th and October 15th).
*   **Auto-Debit Setup:** Set up using secure e-NACH / e-mandate registration during onboarding.

## 2. Early Repayments

Customers can make manual prepayments or clear their entire outstanding balance at any time.
*   **No Prepayment Fees:** There are absolutely zero charges or penalties for paying off the plan early.
*   **How to Pay Early:** Prepayments can be made manually via the FlexiPay App using UPI (GPay, PhonePe, Paytm), Netbanking, or debit card.
*   **Adjustments:** Early repayments automatically reduce the upcoming auto-debit requirement.

## 3. Refunds and Returns

If a customer returns a product purchased using FlexiPay to the merchant:
*   **Merchant Approval:** The refund request must first be approved and processed by the partner merchant in accordance with their return policy.
*   **FlexiPay Adjustment:** Once the merchant notifies FlexiPay of the return, the active plan is adjusted:
    *   If the refund is **full**, the FlexiPay plan is closed, and any installments already paid are refunded back to the customer's bank account within **5 to 7 business days**.
    *   If the refund is **partial**, the refund amount is deducted from the remaining outstanding installments. If the refund exceeds the outstanding balance, the excess is credited back to the customer.

## 4. Missed Payments and Collections

*   **3-Day Grace Period:** If an auto-debit fails, customers have a 3-day grace period to make a manual payment before any fee is charged.
*   **Late Fee Waiver:** A late fee of ₹199 applies on the 4th day after the due date, but this late fee is **automatically waived on the customer's very first missed payment** as a gesture of goodwill.
*   **Support Line:** If experiencing financial difficulty, customers can email support@flexipay.in to discuss custom restructuring options before default reporting.
